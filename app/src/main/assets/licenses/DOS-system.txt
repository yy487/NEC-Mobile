PC-98 DOS resources in NEC Mobile

The standard build uses FreeDOS(98). KERNEL.SYS and SYS.COM come from
lpproj/fdkernel release test-20220120-cherrypick, distributed in
fd98_2hd144_20220123.zip. KERNEL.SYS is copyright (C) 1995-2022
Pasquale J. Villani and The FreeDOS Project and (C) 2001-2022
the FreeDOS(98) porting project.
https://github.com/lpproj/fdkernel/releases/tag/test-20220120-cherrypick

COMMAND.COM is the Japanese PC-98 build from lpproj/freecom_dbcs2
release dbcswip-20220117. It is the nec98/japanese/command.upx binary
in freecom_dbcswip-20220117-bin.zip.
Copyright (C) 1994-2005 Tim Norman and others.
Copyright (C) 2001 SUZU, Silphire.
Copyright (C) 2002 tori.
The PC-98 DBCS edition includes changes by lpproj and its contributors.
https://github.com/lpproj/freecom_dbcs2/releases/tag/dbcswip-20220117

The kernel and command shell are licensed under GPL version 2 or later.
Their license text is available in FreeDOS-GPL.txt. The matching upstream
source archives are provided under third_party/freedos98 of NEC Mobile.
https://github.com/yy487/NEC-Mobile

The templates use the FreeDOS floppy boot record and the hard-disk IPL
and boot record from the same FreeDOS(98) release. The IPL is described
as public-domain code by the upstream BTNPART.TXT notice. NEC Mobile
prepared a compact FAT16 hard disk and a minimal boot floppy. The app
writes the launch script into a separate game-specific copy.

Local builds may also include the builder's PC-98 MS-DOS 2.1, 3.3D,
5.0A and 6.2 startup disks, MS-DOS 5.00 HDI, and MS-DOS 6.2 drivers
and maintenance programs supplied by the builder. These retain their
original Microsoft and NEC copyright notices.
