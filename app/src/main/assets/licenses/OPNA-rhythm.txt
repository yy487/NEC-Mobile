OPNA rhythm PCM resources

The six WAV files are copied from the M88 2.12a distribution supplied for this
project. Original names: 2608_BD.WAV, 2608_SD.WAV, 2608_TOP.WAV, 2608_HH.WAV,
2608_TOM.WAV, and 2608_RYM.WAV.

NEC Mobile stores their names in lowercase and supplies the RYM sample under
the standard RIM name. The PCM content is retained. M88 and FMGEN notices
accompany the application in M88-original-readme.txt and M88-FMGEN-readme.txt.
